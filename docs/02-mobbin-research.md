and why this boat_. Searches were adapted to match.

## Design decisions grounded in the research

1. **Booking is a request, not a checkout.** Every reference with live payments has inventory
   behind it. Here the honest pattern is the Airbnb date-cards + summary, ending in
   _Send via WhatsApp_ or _Send by email_ with every detail pre-filled.
2. **Price is always visible** ("From US$140") in cards, sticky card and mobile bottom bar,
   as on GetYourGuide/Tripadvisor. It was buried mid-page on the old site.
3. **Departure days are first-class data**, shown as chips (Sun · Wed · Fri). They were the
   most repeated fact on the old site.
4. **Typography + one illustrated motif** instead of stock photos. The river route from the
   logo becomes the brand device (hero map, itinerary spine, section dividers).
