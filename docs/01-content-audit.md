- Describes itself as a mid-range cruise operator.
- Boat seats 40 passengers, usually runs with empty seats.
- Each boat: life vests for all passengers, modern toilets, comfortable seats, tables.
- English-speaking host on board.
- Phone / WhatsApp: +66 95 507 3264. Email: adisak.star@gmail.com. Replies within 24 hours.
- Hours: 09:00–20:00. Location: Chiang Khong, Chiang Rai 57140, Thailand (Houay Xai immigration).

### Downstream cruise — Houay Xai → Pak Beng → Luang Prabang

- Departures Sunday, Wednesday, Friday. 2 days.
- Day 1: hotel pick-up after breakfast, pier transfer, luggage handling, border/visa help,
  tea & coffee on board, rural village stop, hot lunch on board, afternoon arrival in Pak Beng,
  hotel transfer, evening free.
- Day 2: breakfast, board, ethnic village stop, lunch on board, Pak Ou cave (many Buddha images),
  elephants bathing and gold panning may be seen, tuk-tuk/minivan transfer to your hotel in Luang Prabang.
- Prices per person, twin/double sharing, incl. daily lunch + Pak Beng night:
  - Standard hotel (A/C, breakfast): US$160 / €150 — single suppl. US$20 / €15
  - Le Grande Pak Beng (pool): US$195 / €185 — single suppl. US$45 / €50
  - Sanctuary Pak Beng Lodge (from 1 Oct 2025): US$195 / €180 — single suppl. US$40
  - Sanctuary summer promotion 20 Apr–30 Sep 2026: US$165 — single US$15 (**expired at audit date**, kept in data with `validTo`, hidden automatically)
- Cruise only (no Pak Beng hotel): THB 4,900 / US$140 / €130.
- Included: pick-up in Chiang Khong or Houay Xai, pier transfers, luggage, visa assistance,
  travel insurance, English-speaking host, daily lunch & refreshments (vegan, vegetarian, halal),
  sightseeing stops incl. Pak Ou cave, Pak Beng hotel of your choice, Luang Prabang pier → hotel transfer.
- Not included: Lao visa fee (paid in US$, plus one passport photo).

### Upstream cruise — Luang Prabang → Pak Beng → Houay Xai

- Departures Monday & Thursday (minimum number of passengers required).
- Day 1: early hotel pick-up, simple breakfast/coffee/tea on board, Pak Ou cave, ethnic Lao village,
  lunch on board, Pak Beng overnight at Mekong Riverside Lodge or BKC Villa (or similar), breakfast incl.
  Premium hotels on request. Pak Beng can be very cold Nov–Jan.
- Day 2: breakfast, lunch on board, one village stop, arrive Houay Xai ~16:30, border transfer
  and bus ticket to Chiang Khong.
- US$190 / €190 per person. Upgrade to Sanctuary or Le Grande at extra cost.
- Can be combined with 2–3 days overland Chiang Khong → Chiang Rai → Chiang Mai.

### Chiang Mai land & cruise (pre-cruise program) — 5 days / 4 nights

- Arrive Chiang Mai Tue → boat Fri; Thu → Sun; Sun → Wed.
- Days 1–2 Chiang Mai, Dusit Princess (4★, 2 min walk to night market), breakfast.
- Day 3 ~300 km to Chiang Khong via hot spring, White Temple, Golden Triangle & Hall of Opium; lunch;
  night at Chiang Khong Teak Garden or Fortune Riverview.
- Days 4–5 downstream cruise with Pak Beng night.
- THB 15,470 / US$455 / €455 per person, minimum 2. Families of 3+: email for pricing.
- Excluded: personal expenses, alcoholic drinks, visa fees.

### Nan → Pak Beng → Luang Prabang — 4 days / 3 nights

- Day 1 (Mon/Wed/Fri) Nan airport, temples, night market. Day 2 markets, villages,
  "chicken coop sauna and massage", northern Thai lunch. Day 3 overland via Pua district to Huay Kon
  border, Lao driver, 55 km (~1 h) to Pak Beng. Day 4 one-day cruise to Luang Prabang.
- **Lao visa must be obtained in advance** (Lao embassy at home or in Bangkok), declaring entry at
  Muang Ngeun, Xayaboury province.
- Includes one-way economy flight Bangkok → Nan (15 kg + 7 kg). Prices: email.

### Stays

- Pak Beng basic: Mon Savahn, Villa Mekong (A/C). Deluxe: Le Grande Pak Beng (~50 villas, pool,
  most river-view with balcony), Sanctuary Pak Beng Lodge (48 rooms, fan superior & A/C deluxe, pool).
- Luang Prabang: Villa Mira, 3★ guest house, 11 rooms (9 superior, 2 family, 27 m²) near Wat Xiengthong.
- Houay Xai: "Homy Dormy" budget stay 5 min from the slow-boat pier, 4 rooms × 4 bunk beds,

### Private charters

  standard equipment. Coffee breaks and lunch on board.

## Issues found on the current site (flag to the owner)

1. **Inconsistent spellings**: Pak Beng/Pak Bang, Houay Xai/Houy Xai/Houi Xai, Luang Prabang/Luang Prbang.
   New site standardises on _Houay Xai, Pak Beng, Luang Prabang_, and on the usual spellings
   of the Nan-route places the source writes as "Pau", "Houy Kone" and "Moung Ngern"
   (Pua, Huai Kon, Muang Ngeun).
2. **Dormitory price "US$1100 per bunk bed"** is almost certainly a typo. Not published — shown as
   "contact for rates" until the owner confirms.
3. **Expired promotion** (Sanctuary summer 2026) still advertised.
4. **Two different addresses** in the contact block.
5. Empty pages in navigation (elephant park, entry procedures, combination page).
6. Photo gallery is behind a member sign-in, so visitors cannot see the boat.
7. Template leftovers: Thai GoDaddy strings, "Add a footnote if this applies to your business",
   "filler@godaddy.com", privacy policy "coming soon".
8. Images have missing or generic alt text; booking relies on email only.
